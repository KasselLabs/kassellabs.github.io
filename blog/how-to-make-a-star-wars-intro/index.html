Make Your Own Star Wars Opening Crawl in Seconds

KL Kassel Labs · November 14, 2025

Originally published on the Kassel Labs blog.

Few things in cinema are as instantly recognizable as that yellow text receding into a field of stars. You hear the fanfare, you read "A long time ago in a galaxy far, far away….", and you know exactly what you're in for.

The good news: you can make your own. With the Star Wars Intro Creator you generate the iconic opening crawl in seconds — no video editing software, no account, no design skills required.

The legendary opening crawl

The crawl debuted in 1977 and has opened nearly every Star Wars film since. George Lucas borrowed the idea from the Flash Gordon serials and Buck Rogers comics he grew up on — a way to hand the audience a chunk of backstory without grinding the movie to a halt. Three or four short paragraphs, tilted into infinity, scored by that unmistakable brass. Simple, and unforgettable.

How to make your own Star Wars intro

  1. Open the creator. Head to the Star Wars Intro Creator.
  2. Set your title and subtitle. The episode line at the top, the subtitle below it — all caps, as tradition demands.
  3. Write your crawl. Three to four short paragraphs. This is where the fun is.
  4. Preview and download. Watch it back, then render the video to download or share.

Want just the title card rather than the full animated crawl? The free Star Wars font generator turns any text into that perspective-tilted yellow title and exports a PNG, instantly.

Tips for a great crawl

  • Keep lines short. Long lines get clipped on smaller screens — aim for 40–50 characters per line.
  • ALL CAPS for the episode title. It's part of the look.
  • Go big. Over-the-top, operatic language is the whole point. Nobody ever wrote a Star Wars crawl that was too dramatic.
  • End on a cliffhanger. Leave them wanting the next episode.
