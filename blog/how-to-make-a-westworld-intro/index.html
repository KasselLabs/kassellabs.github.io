Make a Westworld Intro With Your Own Title

KL Kassel Labs · April 10, 2026

Originally published on the Kassel Labs blog.

A skeletal android is strung onto a milling machine. Tendons are printed, a horse assembles itself mid-gallop, and two artificial hands play a real piano. Westworld's opening — directed by Patrick Clair, the mind behind the True Detective and Daredevil titles — is one of the most philosophically rich sequences in television, and it won the Emmy for Outstanding Main Title Design in 2017.

With the Westworld Intro Creator you can set your own title against it.

The title sequence that asked deep questions

Set to Ramin Djawadi's piano arrangement, the sequence is a meditation on creation, consciousness, and the thin line between humanity and artificiality — hosts being assembled and sculpted, creation watching itself being created. It promises a show about makers and the things they make. Which, if you're putting your own name into it, is pleasingly recursive.

How to make your own Westworld intro

  1. Open the creator. Access westworldintrocreator.kassellabs.io.
  2. Enter your custom title. Replace the default text with your own.
  3. Preview your intro. The live preview shows your title in place.
  4. Render and download. MP4, ready for use.

Ideas for your Westworld intro

  • Tech projects and product demos (the aesthetic is "we build things")
  • Podcast openers with gravitas
  • Creative portfolios
  • Personal projects that deserve a cinematic front door
