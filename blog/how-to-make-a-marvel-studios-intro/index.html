Open Like the MCU: Make Your Own Marvel Studios Intro

KL Kassel Labs · February 6, 2026

Originally published on the Kassel Labs blog.

The comic pages flip past, the fanfare builds, and the red wordmark fills the screen. The Marvel Studios logo sequence is one of the most watched pieces of film in cinema history — the moment every MCU experience actually starts. Fans famously pause it frame by frame to spot their favorite characters in the flipping panels.

With the Marvel Studios Intro Creator that wordmark can say whatever you want.

The logo that opens every Marvel film

The sequence works double duty: it's studio branding, and it's an homage to the source material — decades of comic art compressed into a few seconds of page-flips before the title lands. That's why swapping in your own name feels so satisfying: the whole apparatus of the MCU appears to be introducing you.

How to make your own Marvel Studios intro

  1. Open the creator. Go to the Marvel Studios Intro Creator.
  2. Enter your text. Your name replaces the wordmark.
  3. Preview in real time. See it exactly as it will render.
  4. Render and download. Your MP4, ready for anything.
