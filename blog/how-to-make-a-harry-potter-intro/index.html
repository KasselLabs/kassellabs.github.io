How to Make a Harry Potter Intro

KL Kassel Labs · March 6, 2026

Want to create a Harry Potter intro with your own text? The Kassel Labs Harry Potter Intro Creator lets you generate a personalized wizarding-world title card in seconds.

The magic of the title sequence

The Harry Potter film series is one of the best-loved franchises in cinema history, and its title cards carry real weight. From John Williams' sweeping "Hedwig's Theme" to the golden letterforms that materialize out of swirling mist and starfields, the look and feel of a Harry Potter title sequence signals wonder, mystery, and the promise of adventure.

Each film in the eight-part series had its own variation — darker films used colder tones, early films leaned into the warm golden palette — but the core visual language remained consistent: magical, grand, cinematic.

How to make your own Harry Potter intro

The Kassel Labs Harry Potter Intro Creator lets you create a personalized title card in the style of the films:

Step 1 — Open the creator

Head to kassellabs.io/intro/harry-potter to open the creator.

Step 2 — Enter your custom title

Type in the name or title you want displayed. This replaces the film title in the sequence — so you could write a name, an event, a house name, or anything else that fits the wizarding aesthetic.

Step 3 — Preview your intro

The live preview shows you exactly what the rendered video will look like.

Step 4 — Render and download

Click Render to generate your video. Download the MP4 file and use it for birthday videos, party openers, school projects, YouTube, or social media.

Ideas for your Harry Potter intro

  • Birthday revelations — "[Name] and the [Age]th Birthday" is a guaranteed crowd-pleaser.
  • House announcements — sorting ceremonies, team reveals, or school project intros.
  • Party invitations — send a video invite that opens like a Hogwarts acceptance letter.
  • Classroom projects — students love presenting with a proper cinematic opener.
