Make a Game of Thrones Intro With Your Own Words

KL Kassel Labs · January 9, 2026

Originally published on the Kassel Labs blog.

A clockwork map unfolds. Towers crank themselves out of the ground, gears turn under kingdoms, and a cello line tells you something enormous is about to happen. The Game of Thrones opening didn't just introduce a show — it changed what TV title sequences were allowed to be.

With the Game of Thrones Intro Creator you can put your own words into that world in seconds.

The intro that changed TV title sequences

Designed by Angus Wall's team at Elastic, the sequence won an Emmy in 2011 — and for good reason. Instead of credits over b-roll, it built a mechanical, animated map of an entire fictional world, one that changed episode to episode depending on where the story went. It made the title sequence part of the storytelling.

How to make your own Game of Thrones intro

  1. Open the creator. Head to the Game of Thrones Intro Creator.
  2. Enter your text. Your name, your family, your kingdom.
  3. Preview it. Watch the map machinery do its thing.
  4. Render and download. MP4, ready to share.

Creative uses

  • Family reunions — every house gets a sigil moment
  • Corporate team intros — departments as kingdoms works disturbingly well
  • Wedding videos — two houses, joined
  • Birthdays — epic style for any age
