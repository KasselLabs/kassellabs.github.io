Put Your Name on the Periodic Table — the Breaking Bad Way

KL Kassel Labs · March 20, 2026

Originally published on the Kassel Labs blog.

Two element boxes glowing green — Bromine and Barium — smoke drifting across a New Mexico time-lapse. Since 2008, that title card has been one of TV's most distinctive visual identities.

With the Breaking Bad Intro Creator you can generate the iconic periodic-table title card in seconds — no design or video editing skills required.

The title sequence that made chemistry cool

The opening was created by Mindy Sellar and the team at Electric Sandbox: element symbols lifted straight from the periodic table, highlighted in that unmistakable green, floating over desert time-lapse footage. It told you everything about the show in five seconds — science, menace, and a name that was also a formula.

How to make your own Breaking Bad intro

  1. Open the creator. Go to breakingbadintrocreator.kassellabs.io — the preview updates live as you type.
  2. Type your text. The tool automatically finds real element symbols in your words and highlights them green, exactly like the original.
  3. Preview it. Check which elements it picked.
  4. Render and download. One click, and the MP4 is yours.

Just want the still title card? The free Breaking Bad font generator renders your text with the element-box treatment and exports a PNG instantly.

Tips for a great Breaking Bad-style title

  • Element-rich names hit harder. Names containing He, Na, Ca, Fe, or Au light up beautifully.
  • Keep it short. The original is two words. Two words is the sweet spot.
  • Let it breathe. The drama is in the timing — don't rush the reveal.

Creative uses

  • A nickname or alias card for your channel
  • A company or product launch teaser
  • Birthday videos ("HenRy — 40")
  • Social posts that stop the scroll
