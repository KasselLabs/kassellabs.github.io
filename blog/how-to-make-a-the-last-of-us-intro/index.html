How to Make a The Last of Us Intro

KL Kassel Labs · March 27, 2026

Want to create your own The Last of Us intro with a custom title? The Kassel Labs Last of Us Intro Creator lets you generate a personalized version of the iconic HBO title sequence in seconds.

The title sequence that captured the end of the world

The Last of Us title sequence — from HBO's 2023 adaptation of the beloved video game — is both beautiful and unsettling. Fungal growth slowly consumes and reshapes everyday imagery: leaves, flowers, and organic textures overtaken by spores and mycelium, all underscored by Gustavo Santaolalla's haunting, sparse guitar score. The sequence communicates the show's central horror — nature reclaiming civilization — before a single scene plays.

Created by Perception, the New York-based design studio behind many of Marvel's title sequences, the Last of Us intro was widely praised as one of the best in recent TV history. It sets an emotional tone that the show spends the rest of its running time earning.

How to make your own Last of Us intro

The Kassel Labs Last of Us Intro Creator lets you create a personalized title card in the style of the HBO sequence:

Step 1 — Open the creator

Head to thelastofusintrocreator.kassellabs.io to open the creator.

Step 2 — Enter your custom title

Type the title you want displayed. You can use a name, a phrase, a project title, or any text that fits the show's haunting aesthetic.

Step 3 — Preview your intro

Watch the live preview to see exactly how your title will look in the finished video.

Step 4 — Render and download

Click Render to generate the video file. Download the MP4 and use it in any project — YouTube videos, presentations, birthday surprises, or social media.
