Make a The Last of Us Title Sequence With Your Own Text

KL Kassel Labs · March 27, 2026

Originally published on the Kassel Labs blog.

Fungal tendrils creep across the frame, slowly consuming and reshaping everything they touch, until the weathered title emerges from the growth. HBO's The Last of Us opening — created by New York design studio Perception — is both beautiful and unsettling, and was widely praised as one of the best TV title sequences in recent memory.

The Last of Us Intro Creator lets you generate a personalized version of it in seconds.

The title sequence that captured the end of the world

What makes the sequence work is restraint: no characters, no action — just cordyceps growth rendered as something almost botanical-documentary, set against that aching guitar theme. The eroded, weathered typography does the rest. It's dread as design.

How to make your own Last of Us intro

  1. Open the creator. Visit thelastofusintrocreator.kassellabs.io.
  2. Enter your text. Your title gets the weathered treatment.
  3. Preview it live. See the render before committing.
  4. Download the MP4. Ready to share.

Prefer a still image? The free The Last of Us font generator renders your text in the eroded serif style and exports a PNG — no signup.
