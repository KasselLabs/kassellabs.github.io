Put Your House in the House of the Dragon Intro

KL Kassel Labs · April 3, 2026

Originally published on the Kassel Labs blog.

Blood flows through carved stone channels, tracing the Targaryen family tree in crimson. Where Game of Thrones opened with a clockwork map of a world, its prequel opens with a bloodline — same universe, same design studio (Elastic), completely different statement.

With the House of the Dragon Intro Creator you can put your own house name at the center of it.

The title sequence that rewrote the Targaryen dynasty

When the 2022 prequel arrived, it had an impossible act to follow — the original's Emmy-winning map sequence. Elastic's answer was to go intimate instead of epic: instead of geography, genealogy. Blood running through the model of old Valyria, linking generation to generation. It established the show as worthy of the legacy while being entirely its own thing.

How to make your own House of the Dragon intro

  1. Open the creator. Navigate to houseofthedragonintrocreator.kassellabs.io.
  2. Enter your custom title. A family name, team name, event, or project.
  3. Preview your intro. The live preview shows the final result.
  4. Render and download. MP4, fire and blood included.

Creative uses

  • Family reunions — "House [Your Surname]" on the big screen
  • Sports teams — claim dominion over your league
  • Office presentations — departments as noble houses
  • Fan projects — tribute videos that look the part
